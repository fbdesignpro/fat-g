# 0032 — The avatar overlay: a model's version and its session's effort drawn over its art

> **Rule:** a model's version, and where its style asks for it its short name, is drawn by the page as text over its avatar at the column and capsule sizes, the session's effort as marks beside it and a fast session's speed as a lightning bolt in their look, never baked into fat-g's reading of the art; the texts come from the provider's own reading of the model's identifiers, the marks from a fixed table of the normalized effort ids, the look from the family's own `family.json` alone — its style, then the avatar's — every value of a closed set, a bounded integer or a hex colour.
>
> - **The texts:** each provider's module reads a model identifier into a version and a short name (`gpt-6.1-sol` → `6.1`, `Sol`; `claude-opus-5-5` → `5.5`, `Opus`), the resolved id first, then the key; none where it reads nothing.
> - **The marks:** one to five dots for Low, Medium, High, Extra high and Max, five starbursts for Ultra, by the level's id and never its place in a model's list; none for any other id, for no effort, and for a sub-agent until its own effort is known.
> - **The fast bolt:** one lightning bolt for a session at the fast speed tier, in the marks' look at a starburst's size, in a place of its own among the version's eight — `fastPosition`, the top left by default; drawn whether or not the marks are, and never for a session its caller does not call fast.
> - **The look:** two styles. The version's: effect (plain, outline, glow, chip), size (6–40 % of the square's width), text and effect colours, effect-colour opacity (0 to 100 %, leaving the text opaque), a shade behind the text (none or a darkening patch, its colour and strength), one of eight places on the edge, which texts are drawn, with Name and Version as the only text visibility controls; four presets — Badge, Glow, Band, Chip — set its look whole. The marks': whether they are drawn, one of four edges (a row along the top or the bottom, a column along a side), their size (3–15 %) and spacing (0–10 %), colour, effect (plain, outline, glow), its colour and opacity (leaving the marks opaque), a shade, and the fast bolt's place.
> - **The layers:** fat-g's defaults, then the family's style, then the avatar's, both from the family's `family.json` (version 7); a later field wins, and a version layer naming a preset replaces the look gathered before it. The preferences hold no style (revision 15).
> - **Where:** resolved by main with the avatar ([0031](0031-avatars.md), item 6) for every catalog model, session, sub-agent and conversation speaker, models no catalog lists included; drawn by the `Avatar` component at the capsule (60 px) and column (152–176 px) sizes alone, never at 30 px or less, with the effort and the speed its caller gives: a session's in force, a draft's or a preset's chosen.
> - **What never holds it:** the Pet, a log, the records, the conversation store and the preferences; the family's file holds the look, the session the effort and the speed.

Amended by the maintainer: a glow's strength in both styles, the manifest at version 4; an override as a difference; the Family default's fixed texts (item 6). Amended again at the maintainer's request: the redundant text visibility switch removed, Name and Version alone deciding matched avatars' texts, the fallback always saying both, and the manifest at version 5. ## Context

- **One image, many versions.** Art that bakes its version in needs new art for each new version, and an unversioned image (a sun for every Sol) cannot say which model it is.
- **Short names for new models.** A model no art suits yet falls to its family's default; drawing its short name and version there tells it apart with no new art.
- **The effort at a glance.** Two sessions on one model at different efforts look alike in the capsules; the effort, drawn as marks over the art, tells them apart.
- **The look must suit the art, and travel with it.** A number that reads on a dark starfield may not on a bright sun; the family's author sets the look, and a family copied elsewhere keeps it.

## Decision

1. **Read by the provider.** Shared code names no provider, so each provider's module carries its rule as a pure function on its definition, reached by main for every model and session it resolves. A rule's result is kept only when it is letters, digits and dots, at most 16 characters.
2. **Marks by id.** The table of marks is keyed by the normalized effort ids the providers' catalogs share, so one level draws alike on every model that offers it. The effort is never part of what main resolves: the same resolved avatar draws two sessions at two efforts, and a change of effort resolves nothing again.
3. **Layers, then presets.** The version's look is merged layer by layer — fat-g's defaults, the family's, the avatar's; a preset in a layer replaces the look before it, and the same layer's fields then adjust it, so choosing Chip on a family that glows gives a chip. The text selections pass through presets untouched. The marks' style merges field by field, with no preset.
4. **Checked before it is drawn.** A style from a manifest is read field by field: a field of its form is kept, any other dropped and named as an `overlay-field` problem in the Avatars tab (`overlay.effort.size`, `avatars.2.overlay.version.size`), the manifest otherwise applied. A style from the page is refused whole if any value is not of its form. The page receives the look resolved and sets it as custom properties alone; no value is ever a CSS string.
5. **Drawn where it reads.** Text and marks are sized as percentages of their square, so one rule fits the 60, 152 and 176 px squares; the type scale's tokens cannot express it, and the stylesheet test names the text as its one exception. Dots are spans; a starburst and the fast bolt are each one fixed SVG shape, so every system draws them alike. Where the marks stand on the text's edge, the text moves inward by their room. A glow reaches as far as its style's glow strength says, 0 to 200 % of fat-g's own, for the text and the marks alike. All are decorative as the art is: no pointer events, no selection, hidden with their avatar from assistive technology.
6. **Edited in the family.** Settings → Avatars' family editor lists the Family default's row above the avatars; the chosen row's style — the version's and the marks' — stands beside them: the Default style, the family's `Default` folder's and every other avatar's starting point, or an avatar's Overrides, each field it sets highlighted with its way back to the default and all of them cleared at once by Reset customizations. An override is a difference: a field equal to what the Default style gives it is dropped from the draft, so one the Default style comes to agree with ends, and the avatar follows it from then on. The `Default` folder always draws the short name and version, by a fixed layer over the Default style that no other avatar inherits: it stands for any model no avatar suits, so the style's Name and Version toggles set the other avatars' texts. Both off hides their text; there is no master visibility switch. A preview draws the chosen row at both sizes with the texts of a model it suits, or one chosen, and an effort chosen. Save family writes the family's `family.json` ([0031](0031-avatars.md), item 10).
7. **The built-in art.** Each provider's Gradient and Lightning families spell the model's short name in letters and no version: the Default style says the version alone, white at the top right with no shade over the art, each family in a look of its own — flat, outlined, glowing or on a chip — and the avatars inherit it with no override; the `Default` folders — the gradient with no letters — say the short name too; the marks stand at the bottom. The Anime families' portraits carry no letters; their Default style also says the version alone, white with a black outline at the top right, and their `Default` folders, a flat colour, say the short name too. The MTX families spell the short name as the Gradient and Lightning art does, say the version in the Anime families' outline, and their `Default` folders, code rain in the provider's colour, say the short name too.
8. **The fast bolt**. A session at the fast speed tier draws one lightning bolt; the caller says whether it is fast, as it gives the effort, and nothing of the speed is resolved by main. The bolt takes the marks' look whole — their size scaled as a starburst's, their colour, effect, effect colour and opacity, glow strength, and shade, a patch from its corner or from the middle of its edge — so the family's author sets one field more: `fastPosition`, one of the version's eight places, the top left by default, the corner no preset gives the version and the marks never take. No place is refused for meeting the text or the marks: the author puts it where the art wants it. Where it stands in the text's own place, the text moves aside by its room — along the edge from a corner, inward from a side's middle, down or up from the top's or the bottom's middle; elsewhere they may meet. It is drawn wherever the overlay is, at the capsule and column sizes alone, whether or not the marks are shown, `show` being the effort's. The family editor sets its place under the effort marks, and its previews draw a fast session, so the place shows.

## Consequences

- A new model of a family whose matches suit it shows its version with no new art, and a model with no art at all shows its short name and version on its family's default.
- A family that relies on the overlay is a manifest at version 7; a user's family at an earlier version is refused whole until its `schemaVersion` and `minReaderVersion` are raised (0014), and stands as its folders meanwhile.
- The fast bolt's place came without a new version: it is a field added beside the others, read with its default where a file has none, skipped without a word by a build that does not know it and kept when that build saves the file. Every build reads every version-6 file alike, so a raise would protect nothing, and would have refused every user's version-6 family whole until edited by hand. This departs from [0014](0014-format-compatibility-starts-at-first-release.md)'s item 3 for such a field alone; a change to what a written field means still raises both numbers.
- A provider whose ids carry no version draws no version until its rule reads one.
- Nothing of an avatar's look is the user's apart from the family's file: deleting the preferences changes no look.

## Revisit when

- The centre is wanted as a place, or another mark is asked for — a speed tier beyond Fast (Ultra), say, which the one bolt cannot tell from Fast.
- A provider's display names, rather than its ids, become the better source of a version.
- A provider adds an effort level the table does not know.
