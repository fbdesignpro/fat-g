# 0029 — Plans: a mode of one prompt, a record of fat-g's own, a decision the user takes

> **Rule:** a plan turn is a mode of one prompt, never of the chat: the Plan toggle sends it, and the chat's mode stands throughout. The plan a provider proposes is a record of fat-g's own in `plans/`, beside the conversation store and under its own envelope: versioned, kept whole, listed in its project's registry, and kept until the user removes it.
>
> - **On Claude:** `set_permission_mode plan` before the prompt, the plan read from the `ExitPlanMode` request's `input.plan` alone, and the request answered by the user's decision. At the turn's end the mode is set back, naming one of the three words a change names or the word of the process's own launch, and no other.
> - **On Codex:** the session's handshake asks for the experimental API, a plan turn's `turn/start` names `collaborationMode` `plan`, and every later turn of a thread that ever planned names `default`. The plan streams to a draft on disk, and the completed text is the version.
> - **The decision:** Execute plan, Cancel or feedback, taken once, from a panel in the input's place. It lives apart from a provider's request: while it waits, a prompt and a change of mode are refused. Where no request holds it, its decisions are turns fat-g starts, and it waits across a restart.
> - **Any plan, any chat:** Review existing plan brings a plan of the project into any of its chats, on either provider, its text given whole in the prompt inside a block of fat-g's.
> - **The review (M22e):** Review by model sends a version, whole in a block of fat-g's, to a reviewer session of its own on the preset the user chooses — the chat's to start with, any account the project is offered to — under its provider's reading mode. Its findings, read from the last `json` block of a review turn's final answer, are a review record beside the plan, marked by the user alone. Integrate sends the accepted ones to a chat that writes the next version, which names its review.
> - **Review entry points withheld:** the chat's Review button and the plan-ready panel's Review by model button are hidden until the maintainer chooses to offer review. The existing implementation and any already-running review remain available to finish.
> - **One workflow, shared:** every chat that made a plan, brought it or received its integration takes part in one workflow kept in the plan's record — ready, under review, being revised, being implemented, interrupted — and decides on its latest complete version. Main admits one operation at a time, reserved before anything is made or sent, against the revision the page drew from. A plan implemented or discarded is deleted; each chat keeps a line saying where it was implemented.
> - **Never:** no plan file a CLI writes is read, nothing is written into a project, and no plan's text or title, nor a finding, an appraisal or a note, reaches a log, the app state or the Pet — but for a session's name that holds its plan's title, kept and shown as any session's name: a reviewer's, and a chat's that had none when the plan was brought into it.

## Context

- **Both CLIs plan, and neither the way the other does.** M22b's spikes traced both (chat-protocols §17 (private research note); the plan-mode research, §2–§6 (private research note)).
  - **Claude** takes `set_permission_mode plan` on a live process. The model writes its plan to a file in the account's configuration folder, with no request, then calls `ExitPlanMode`, whose request carries the text in `input.plan`. A `deny` with words revises it, an `allow` returns the CLI to its earlier mode within the same turn, and a `deny` with `interrupt` ends the turn. Under Read only the model has no `Write`, so the request comes empty.
  - **Codex** plans when `turn/start` names `collaborationMode` `plan`, which needs the experimental API on the handshake. The plan streams as `item/plan/delta` and completes as a `plan` item. No request waits on it. The mode stays with the thread — through a turn that names none, a Stop and a resume — until a turn names `default`.
- **What M100 left:** the Plan toggle and the Review button, drawn and doing nothing, and a `plan` kind reserved in M21's registry that nothing raised.
- **The maintainer's brief:** a plan kept by fat-g whatever the provider, its text in the pane, its decisions in a panel, and later a review by another model (M22e). Their answers made a project's plans a registry independent of their chats, transferable across providers and accounts by design, so that one model may evaluate another's plan.
- **The review loop (M22e).** M22e's spikes ran a review on both CLIs (chat-protocols §19 (private research note)). Every answer, a later round's too, closed with one fenced `json` block that matched a schema given in the prompt alone, so no CLI schema option is used. A note that settles a point brought the whole review again, a moved finding under a new id naming its earlier one in `was`; a note that only asks was answered in prose above the review sent again unchanged. Codex sends a `commentary` message before its reads, and its final answer is labelled `final` as it arrives; Claude's end names its answer by id.

## Decision

1. **A plan turn is a mode of one prompt.** `chat:send` carries `mode: 'plan'`; the chat's mode is never changed for it, and the header shows the chat's mode throughout. A successful Execute plan or Cancel releases the Plan toggle, saving that choice with the draft so follow-ups are ordinary prompts; a refusal or failure leaves it pressed. Feedback leaves the toggle as chosen. A provider's descriptor declares the modes a plan turn may start under (`capabilities.plans`) and why the others cannot, and its facet's `plans()` says whether the account takes one. Claude plans under Ask first, Edits ok, Auto reviewed and Berserk, never Read only, which cannot save a plan; Codex under all five. A plan turn is a turn as any other; fat-g adds no notice at its start. A turn fat-g starts to carry one on — an async question's answer given during it — is a plan turn too, with the plan fields of the turn it answers.
2. **On Claude.**
   - **Into plan mode:** before the prompt, `set_permission_mode plan`, its reply confirmed. The checks of [0020](0020-claude-chat-sessions.md), item 8 hold to the mode in force with the target of a change that waits beside it, never to whether a turn runs: `plan` from that change, and the chat's mode again from the moment Execute's `allow` is written, armed before that write.
   - **The tool:** `ExitPlanMode` is listed in every mode that plans, and `EnterPlanMode` in none. Its request outside a plan turn, or after this turn's Execute, is refused as any unknown request is.
   - **The text:** from `input.plan` alone. A request with no plan is denied with fixed words saying none reached fat-g, and a notice. A request whose line passes the 4 MiB a session carries is refused by its id with a notice that the plan was too large, its tool's name read from the line's first 512 bytes beside its kind and id. No plan file is read, nor deleted.
   - **The three answers**, to the one pending request: feedback is `deny` with the user's text after fat-g's request (item 7), kept as the user's message at the end of the line's turn, naming the version to revise, in the write that decides the line; Execute is `allow` with the request's input as it came and no `updatedPermissions`; Cancel is `deny` with `interrupt: true` and fixed words, and its turn reads stopped, as a Stop's does.
   - **Out of plan mode:** at the turn's end, wherever the process is still in `plan` — after a Cancel, a Stop or a plan the model left — the mode is set back to the chat's. That send may name one of the three words a change between modes names, or, coming out of `plan` alone, the word the process was launched under, Berserk's `bypassPermissions` included, checked on the wire against that launch and never against a frame. A reply that names another word blocks Send as a start that reports another policy does.
3. **On Codex.**
   - **The handshake:** a session's asks for the experimental API and keeps `item/plan/delta` beside the message's delta, opting out of the nine others. The secretary's, the sandbox's and the existing connection's are unchanged.
   - **The turn:** a plan turn's `turn/start` names `collaborationMode` `plan` with the record's model, its effort and null instructions. Every later turn of a thread that ever ran a plan turn of fat-g's names `default` the same way — Execute, a plain prompt, after a Stop, a restart or a resume — read from the store's record of each turn, so the reset is named again until Codex has taken it. A thread that never planned names none, and an aside's fork never does.
   - **The text:** a `plan` item's `item/started` opens a draft on disk; each delta is appended to it, flushed at a message's progress interval, at a Stop and at the quit; `item/completed` gives the whole text, which is the version. A plan whose completion fat-g did not receive whole is `unfinished`, kept with what came, and never decided.
   - **The decisions are turns fat-g starts:** feedback is a plan turn whose prompt is the user's text after fat-g's request (item 7); Execute is a turn under the chat's mode whose prompt is "Implement the plan you proposed."; each carries the plan's block, empty (item 7). Cancel sends nothing.
   - **Best effort:** where Codex refuses the handshake that asks for the experimental API, the process is opened again with the plain one, and plans read unavailable for that account until fat-g restarts; a `turn/start` refused while it names `collaborationMode` does the same. A thread an earlier run left in plan mode, where plans are now off, gets a notice saying so once.
   - **Skip** on a blocking question replies "No answer" under each of its questions, and the facet declares the decline.
4. **The plan store: `plans/<plan id>/`, main's alone.**
   - **The record:** `plan.json` under the version envelope (schema 1; 2 since M22e, for item 16's integration), unknown fields kept: the plan's id, its project, its title and when it was made, and per version its number, its state — `drafting`, `complete` or `unfinished` — the session, turn, provider, account and model that made it, its time, a complete version's length and SHA-256, its cause (a plan turn, feedback or, since M22e, an integration) and the version it revises. The text is `v<N>.md`, or `v<N>.draft.md` while drafting or unfinished.
   - **No length of fat-g's:** a version is the whole text received. What crosses to the page is bounded: a push of at most 64 KiB, a read of a range of at most 1 MiB with the whole length and the hash, and a tab that draws a version whole up to 256 KiB and by sections beyond it.
   - **Ordered for a crash:** a version's file is whole on disk before `plan.json` names it complete. A plan's removal renames its folder to `.removing-<plan id>`, then deletes it. A project's removal writes `.removing-<project id>.json`, naming every plan it takes, before anything else, and deletes them after the records' write, the mark last; the mark is lifted where that write fails. At launch, what `plan.json` does not name and a folder with no `plan.json` are deleted, a `drafting` version becomes `unfinished`, a plan's mark is finished, and a project's mark is read against the records — finished for a project no longer listed, lifted for one still listed — with nothing deleted while the records cannot be read.
   - **The registry** of a project's plans is read from `plans/` alone and never from a chat, so a plan stays listed, opened and removable after every chat that made it is gone.
   - **The one writer of `plans/`**, free of Electron, providers and sessions, reached by the sessions through an injected interface.
5. **The plan in the conversation** ([0019](0019-conversation-store.md), version 7; 8 since M22e). A plan's line is an `interaction` item of body kind `plan`: its plan, its version, whether it was made in the chat or brought, whether a request holds it, its outcome — `writing`, `pending`, `feedback`, `executed`, `cancelled`, `withdrawn`, `dropped` or `unfinished` — and when it was decided and settled. It holds no text. A prompt item may carry its plan's fields — the plan, the version, `revise` or `execute`, or since M22e `review`, which revises nothing — and a turn whether it planned. At a launch a line a request held reads `dropped`, a line no request holds stays `pending`, and a `writing` one is `unfinished`.
6. **The decision.**
   - **Elapsed time pauses** while a turn waits for its own blocking plan decision, approval or question, and resumes once it is settled. Overlapping requests count as one wait. The saved duration excludes these waits on main's monotonic clock; an async question or another worker's request does not pause the parent turn. A plan awaiting a decision after its turn ended adds no elapsed time (the maintainer).
   - **Its lifetime is the line's,** not a request's. Claude's `ExitPlanMode` alone raises a request of the registry's `plan` kind, and its line waits exactly while the request does. A Codex plan's line waits from its completion, and a brought plan's from its bringing, through a turn's end and a restart, until a decision.
   - **Taken once:** `plans:decide` is taken only while the line is `pending` and, where a request holds it, while that request is pending on the session's current process; anything else is refused `not-pending` and sends nothing. A decision that starts a turn is written in the prompt's own transaction.
   - **While it waits,** `chat:send` is refused `plan-pending`, the chat's mode cannot change, and the panel stands in the composer's place. When the panel appears, its plan's artifact tab opens and is selected at that version, including after returning to the chat or restarting; subsequent renders leave the user's tab choice alone (the maintainer). The session's attention is `plan`, said "PLAN READY", raised in READY's place at a turn's end, and at a launch that finds the line still waiting in the chat that made it (the maintainer's word). Every chat of a ready plan reads it, a brought one's too, at a launch and in a run (item 21).
   - **What a chat may offer:** Cancel always; Execute wherever the chat can take a prompt, but not under the provider's reading mode, which can carry out nothing; and feedback only under a mode that takes a plan turn. Each is disabled with its reason where it is not offered, and refused by main before anything is consumed. To execute a plan that waits under the reading mode, the user cancels it, chooses a mode that acts and brings it back (the maintainer's visual review).
7. **The chain and the block.**
   - **A version's plan:** a version that arrives in a turn is the next version of the plan that turn's newest plan line names — refused where that line was executed — else of the plan its prompt's fields name to revise, else the first of a new plan, titled by its first heading or its prompt's first line, its Markdown marks dropped and normalized as every name is ([0011](0011-records-store.md)). A second plan in one Codex turn is that plan's next version, and the first line is withdrawn as changed.
   - **The block:** every prompt fat-g composes for a plan's decision carries an opening and a closing line naming the plan, the version and a token of twelve random hex digits that no text of the plan holds, so that no plan can close it. A plan's own chat's block is empty; a brought plan's holds the version's whole text, from the plan store. The prompt is kept as it was sent, drawn as its words with one folded line for the block, and a Codex history's user message holding a block is that plan's prompt where the store has no line for it.
   - **A feedback's request** *(the maintainer; clarified)*: the user's feedback, on every route, follows fixed words of fat-g's asking the model to acknowledge the specific feedback first, in a sentence or two, and say what it will investigate or change; investigate it and propose the complete revised plan; then summarize which feedback it integrated, what changed and why, and anything it did not incorporate and why. Where proposing the plan pauses the turn for the user's decision, the summary comes immediately before the proposal. The acknowledgement and summary belong in the chat, outside the plan's text. The page identifies the request by its first sentence and draws it as the user's words, with a fold "Feedback as sent" holding fat-g's words and the user's as they were sent; the block's fold stands where the prompt carries a block.
8. **Any plan, any chat.** Review is a menu whose item is Review existing plan: the project's plans of every provider, account and model, newest first. `plans:bring` names the plan, the version and its hash as the list showed them; main checks that the plan is the chat's project's, the version complete with that hash, and the chat able to take a prompt with no decision waiting. The line then stands in a turn of fat-g's own, never sent, and its decisions are turns fat-g starts: Execute is "Implement the plan in the block below." and the block; feedback is fat-g's words asking for a revision, the block, then the user's text after fat-g's request (item 7), and its plan the plan's next version. It reads PLAN READY as every chat of a ready plan does (item 21), and a chat that has no name is named `Plan: <title>` as the plan is brought in: a `generated` name, which a name the user types replaces, and which none of fat-g's own turns for the plan asks the secretary to better (the maintainer).
9. **Removal settles what waits.** A plan is removed from its tab's menu after a confirmation naming how many chats wait on it; each waiting decision is then cancelled — a held Claude request answered as Cancel answers it — each written before the next, and the folder is marked only after. A plan is not removed while one of its versions is written. A project's removal settles its plans' decisions, marks once and takes its plans; a session's leaves them. Settings → Storage shows the plans' count and size, shows the folder, and removes all.
10. **Six channels of the Main window's alone:** `plans:read`, `plans:list`, `plans:remove`, `plans:decide`, `plans:bring` and `plans:storage`, with `shell:showDataFolder` naming the plans folder, and the push `plans:changed`. Main logs each outcome's kind and counts, never a text, a title or a path.
11. **The page.** Plan, where the chat cannot take a plan turn, is drawn unpressed and dimmed as every unavailable control is, its tooltip saying why, and the user's choice is kept for where the chat plans. The plan's line in the chat, slightly indented, names the plan in a box with a light bulb — inverted, or dashed in the page's own colours with "being written…" while a Codex plan is written — its version where there are several, when it was made, whether it was brought and what was decided, and holds no decision. The provider and model are omitted beside this button (the maintainer). *(The maintainer's reference:)* the panel is two columns: the same box naming the plan and the version a decision stands on, over the feedback field, "Send feedback / notes & update plan"; beside them Leave plan mode, keep plan with Discard plan as a trash button, Review by model (item 12), and Execute, which runs under the chat's mode, named in the menu above; nothing in it takes the focus on arrival, and Escape decides nothing. The `plan` tab kind shows a version chosen among the chain's, streams a Codex plan as it is written, and says where a decision waits on another version. Explain this works on a plan's text, as an aside's source of kind `plan`.
12. **A review (M22e).** Review by model, on any plan's panel, opens the model dialog ([0016](0016-model-presets.md)): the plan and the version, Recents and Presets above the model chooser on the chat's own preset, and for the selection as it stands the reviewer, that the review is a session of its own spending that account's usage, and what its reading mode still does. the reviewer's preset is the user's choice, of any provider and account the project is offered to, checked as a new chat's start checks one; a reviewer on another provider than the chat's runs under that provider's reading mode. Start review:
    - **checks first:** the chosen preset is one a new chat could start on; the version waits in the chat, is complete and is its plan's newest complete one, else `stale`; the chosen account is connected and its provider's reading mode usable. Nothing is made before these pass, and nothing of the plan's chat is needed, so a plan whose chats are gone is reviewed as well;
    - **makes the reviewer:** a session of the plan's project on the chosen provider, account, model and effort, selected, named "Review of Plan: <title> v<N>" as a `generated` name ([0028](0028-secretary.md)), its chat's mode set to the provider's reading mode, as the mode control sets one before a process. A refused mode removes it again;
    - **writes the review `running`,** then sends fat-g's composed prompt: the instruction (version 2, K2's words), the plan's title and version, the version's whole text in the plan's block (item 7), the project's folder and the findings' JSON Schema. Its fields name the plan with the intent `review`, so the page folds its block, and the chain revises nothing for it. It names no session and asks no secretary. A refused send fails the review `refused`.

    The reviewer is an ordinary session from then on: the user may talk in it, rename it, ask asides in it or remove it, which fails a running review `stopped`.
    While the review's triage panel stands — its latest round live, its panel shown, no turn of it running or asking — the reviewer's attention is `review`, said "PLAN REVIEW", in READY's place, raised again at a launch that finds it so; Cancel, Send notes, an integration sent or a stale review lets go of it. Kinds alone reach the summary.
    *(The maintainer:)* a latest round with no finding holds, in the panel's place of its counts and action, "No findings: the plan passes this review." and a green "Great! Take me back to the plan": it leaves the panel as Cancel does, selects the chat where that version's decision waits — the one the review started from first — else the version's own chat, opens the plan's tab there and marks the reviewer's chat Done; the decision itself stays the user's. Nothing new is stored: the plan's line and tab say "reviewed: no findings" from the round's empty list.
13. **A review's turns.** Only the turns fat-g starts for a review are read — its first and each of Send notes' — each once its end is written, by the turn's last `final` assistant message of the session's own, on either provider, under fat-g's own item id.
    - **The block:** the last `json` fence of that answer, an unclosed one read to the message's end as CommonMark reads it. The prompt's schema asks a strict contract: an id `T<tier>.<n>` whose digit is its tier, given once; a tier, a category, a title, an example, a description and a correction; an optional anchor and `was`; the instruction's version; an appraisal on one line; and lengths — 32 findings, a title of 200, a field of 600, an anchor's heading and quote of 200 each. *(The maintainer:)* those lengths are asked, and none is a reason to refuse: a block is read past every one of them, the appraisal of any length, the cut every message of a chat has bounding the whole. The appraisal's line breaks are folded, a field the contract does not hold is dropped, and so is an anchor or a `was` that does not read. A finding that does not read — no object, no such id, a tier its id does not name, an unknown category, a missing or empty text, or an id a finding before it gave — is set aside, and the rest of the block is the round. A block brings no round only where it is no JSON, no object, of another version, holds no list, or holds findings none of which reads.
    - **What it brings:** a block that reads is a round. *(The maintainer:)* in a later round, a finding the answer set aside that the round before held, and that no finding the answer gave holds or names in `was`, stands as the round before had it, its mark and note carried. The findings list says, in fat-g's words under the appraisal, which findings were left out and which stand as before; a failed review, and an answer that brought no updated one, name what was wrong with the block, read again from the answer — never a text of the model's but a finding's id. On a first answer, none, several or one that does not read fails the review with that reason; on a later answer the latest round stands, and a line under the answer says so. A turn that ends with no answer, failed or stopped does the same. A review an earlier build failed for a length keeps its reason, `over-bound`.
    - **A turn that asks:** one whose answer carries a card's questions is `asked`, and the turn the card's answer starts continues it, read as the asked turn would have been. A dismissed or passed card ends it with no answer. Claude's questions hold their turn and change nothing here.
    - **The plain composer:** a turn the user starts in the reviewer's chat is none of the review's, whatever its answer holds.
14. **The review record:** `plans/<plan id>/reviews/<review id>.json`, schema 2 under the version envelope, unknown fields kept, written by the plan store alone, each change one atomic write of the whole file.
    - **It holds:** the reviewed version and its hash, the chat that started it, the instruction's version, the reviewer's session and preset; each turn started for it and what it brought; the rounds, each with its turn, its answer's item, its appraisal and findings as checked and, once the next came, its marks frozen; the live marks, notes and revisions; whether the panel stands; `staleBy`; and the integrations (item 16). The composed texts are not kept: the plan's text is the plan store's, a prompt the conversation's.
    - **Stale:** a review is stale once its plan holds a complete version numbered above the one reviewed — written into each older review at that version's commit, after `plan.json`, into a review as it is created where one already exists, and derived again at launch. A version being written or left unfinished makes none stale. A stale review keeps its list, its marks and its notes, with no panel.
    - **At launch:** a review left `running` and a turn left `waiting` or `asked` end `interrupted`; an integration left `sent` is settled from `plan.json` — `integrated` by a complete version naming it, else `interrupted`, a drafting or unfinished one settling nothing. Nothing is sent again. A review fat-g cannot read, or written by a newer reader, is left as it is, and a plan whose `plan.json` is refused keeps its folder, its reviews included, whole.
    - **Removal:** a review goes with its plan, its project and Remove all, and never with its session. Settings → Storage counts the reviews beside the plans.
15. **The marks are the user's.**
    - **Marking:** a finding is open, accepted or refused only at the user's press — a row's click, its Accept or Refuse, a tier's Accept all or Refuse all, Space on a row — and a click that ends in a selection marks nothing. Each mark is written at its press, and a note of at most 2,000 units as it is typed.
    - **Send notes,** while a finding is undecided and a note is written, sends a turn of the reviewer's: fat-g's words, each noted finding with its mark and note, every finding's mark, the round's rules and the schema. The notes it sent leave their fields.
    - **Carrying:** the next round's marks and unsent notes carry from the finding its `was` names, where no other finding of the round claims that id, else from the one with its own id; a finding whose words changed reads revised, a new one is open, and one no new finding came from leaves the list. Earlier rounds are inert.
    - **Never fat-g's:** no round, no model and nothing of fat-g's marks a finding.
    - **Anchors and Explain:** an anchor opens the plan's tab at the reviewed version, its passage found by main by its heading, then the quote, or the version from its start with a word that it was not found. Explain on a finding is an aside of the reviewer's on the finding's words ([0025](0025-asides.md), item 2).
16. **Integration.**
    - **Offered once every finding is decided and one is accepted.** The targets are the plan's own chat, the chat that started the review, the reviewer and a new session, each by its route or with why not, and no mode is changed for an offer. *(the maintainer:)* the page offers two of them, "Integrate using…" the chat that created the plan or a new chat, each a plain row, the plan's chat dimmed with why on hover where it has no route; the reviewer's own chat, where the popup stands, which under its reading mode writes no plan, the chat the review started from and the plan's other chats, which main still lists, are not offered there. Choosing a new chat is one way: the target popup gives way to the model dialog on the reviewer's preset, whose Integrate makes the new session on the selection, checked as a new chat's start checks one, its route worked out for that provider and account at the press; its Cancel sends nothing.
    - **The routes:** `decision`, the feedback of the reviewed version's pending decision — a Claude request's `deny`, or, where no request holds the line, a plan turn, offered only where the chat's mode takes one — and `plan-turn`, a composed plan turn whose block holds the version's whole text, fields `revise`.
    - **The text:** fat-g's instruction to evaluate the findings and write the next version — saying first what it takes from them, and just before the revised plan what it changed and why and which findings it did not take and why, without listing them again (the maintainer) — the plan's title and version, the accepted findings with the note in each one's field, and the refused ones that have a note; at most 100,000 units, the route adding the block. A note already sent with Send notes is not sent again.
    - **The order:** main checks again, a new target session is made first, the integration is written `sent` naming its round, its target, its route and the turn it allots, and then it is sent. A refused send ends it and removes an unstarted new session again. One integration of a review is in flight at a time.
    - **Provenance:** a version made in that turn, or in one a card's answer started to continue it, is the plan's next, its cause `integration`, naming its review, its integration and the findings it took, and revising the reviewed version.
    - **Its end:** a complete version naming it settles it `integrated`; it ends `no-plan` where its turn ends with none, `withdrawn` or `dropped` with Claude's request, `refused` at its send, and the panel comes back. Nothing is sent again by itself.
    - **On the line and the tab:** the plan's line and tab say the state of the reviews of their version, and the tab's head names each reviewer.
    - **In the chat** *(the maintainer)*: a prompt that carries an integration, in whichever chat, is one folded line, "Plan feedback: <ids> accepted; <ids> refused", unfolding to the prompt as sent, as a review's first prompt is. It is known by the instruction's first sentence, which every wording keeps. A Claude request that takes it keeps it as the user's message after the line (item 2). The instruction asks for a word on the findings first and a summary just before the revised plan.
17. **Reviews and privacy.**
    - **Nothing without a press:** no review starts, no round is asked for and nothing is integrated by itself, and no timer of fat-g's waits on a review.
    - **Kept apart:** prompts and answers are in the conversation store, and reviews, rounds, marks, notes and integrations in `plans/<id>/reviews/`. No finding, appraisal, note or composed text reaches a log, the app state or the Pet, and logs count kinds.
    - **The one exception** is a session's name that holds the plan's title: the reviewer's, and a chat's that had none when the plan was brought into it, `Plan: <title>`. It is kept in `records.json` and shown in the Main window as any session's name, and never reaches a log or the Pet, which shows no session. It outlives the plan: deleting the plan leaves it, until the session is renamed or removed.
    - **Eight channels of the Main window's alone:** `reviews:start`, `reviews:read`, `reviews:mark`, `reviews:note`, `reviews:send`, `reviews:explain`, `reviews:panel` and `reviews:integrate`, each schema-checked, a review or session of another project answered as unknown; and the push `reviews:changed`, of ids alone.

### The shared workflow (the maintainer's plan)

18. **One workflow for each plan,** in its `plan.json` (version 3): its state — `ready`, `reviewing`, `revising`, `implementing`, `interrupted`, or `implemented` or `cancelled` on the way to deletion — a revision counting its changes, the latest complete version every decision stands on, the chats taking part, and the operation that holds it: its kind (`review`, `revision`, `implementation`), its session, the version it started from with its hash, the turns it went by, and for a review or an integration its review. A chat takes part when it makes the plan, brings it, or receives its integration; a reviewer only reviews, and takes part by integrating into itself. One coordinator in main (`src/main/sessions/workflow.ts`) admits, holds and settles every operation; the store keeps the record, and the page draws a chat's part from `plans:workflow` alone, never from its own newest line, which is history.
19. **One operation at a time.** Execute plan, feedback, Review by model and Integrate are each reserved synchronously in main — written into the record, or for a review being made and an integration's target being made, held in memory — before anything is made or sent; a decision names the plan and the revision its panel was drawn from, and one from an older state, or one another operation holds, is refused `stale` or `locked` with the workflow as it now stands, and sends nothing. A version is written into a plan only where the workflow admits it — from the turns of the revision a chat holds, or a second plan of the turn that wrote the current version — and a turn whose plan was removed writes none. A version's commit readies the plan on it in the same write.
20. **Every chat on the newest version.** When a version is ready, every chat's older lines read `superseded`, and a Claude request still held on an older version is answered as Cancel answers it, its turn ending, before any decision is taken again. Before another chat carries the plan out, every other chat's request on it is retired the same way and its line reads `elsewhere`. A chat that holds no decision on the current version — its request retired, or the version written elsewhere — sends that version's whole text in its block; only a chat's own native request, or the version its own thread wrote, is decided without it.
21. **What a chat shows.** Ready: the panel — Execute, **Discard plan** (a trash button), **Leave plan mode, keep plan**, Review by model and feedback. Under review: "Plan under review", the reviewer's chat and Cancel review, in the composer's place. Being revised: "Plan being revised" and the writer, in the composer's place, but in the writer itself. Being implemented: a line above the composer saying where, ordinary conversation going on. Interrupted: the same line, with Discard plan; in the chat that carried it, its next ordinary prompt continues the implementation, and a plan turn there is refused. Implemented: the plan deleted, each chat keeps a line "Plan implemented in [chat]" (a conversation's `planNote`, version 11) until it takes part in another plan. In the implementing chat, that composer line disappears after its first later turn ends, including after reopening the chat; the stored note is retained. A chat's mode may change while its plan is ready, its request on it retired first (`retired`), the decision standing on the same version; it may not while the plan is under review or being revised. Plan is released in every chat's saved draft when the plan is carried out or discarded. A plan's tab follows its newest complete version, but where the user chose a version in its chooser. *(The maintainer:)* every chat of the plan says its state in its capsule and status bar. While the plan is ready each reads PLAN READY. While a chat or the reviewer holds it, the others read "Waiting on plan review", "Waiting on plan revision" or "Waiting on plan implementation" — an interrupted implementation's too — black on `--color-waiting`, an orange apart from the attention yellow, and the chat holding it shows its own turn. The wait is a kind in the session's live summary (`planWait`), no attention: nothing acknowledges it and nothing announces it, and the chat's own question, approval, error, stop or running turn stands before it. A chat that has run nothing is drawn as a started one while it takes part in a plan. A plan ready in several chats at once is announced once, naming them. Bringing an existing plan through Review is user-initiated: its PLAN READY stays lit without flashing in that chat, in both the Main window and Pet; a subsequent plan result uses the normal attention behavior.
22. **A review is a hard stop.** It holds the plan until it is cancelled — from the reviewer or any chat of the plan, a turn of it stopped and its end awaited first — concluded with no change (no finding, or every finding refused: Take me back to the plan), or handed to an integration, each closing it for good (`closed`, review record version 2; there is no hiding it, and `reviews:panel` is replaced by `reviews:close`). Cancelling or concluding marks the reviewer Done; an integration does once its target took it, but where the reviewer integrates into itself. While it is open the reviewer takes no prompt of the user's own; a card's answer and fat-g's composed prompts go on. A review whose first run fails closes it as failed, the plan ready again with the failure kept. Removing, shelving or marking an open reviewer Done cancels its review. One review of a plan is open at a time; later ones are records of their own.
23. **Integration.** Its hand-off changes `reviewing` to `revising` in one write, the target taking part; every chat of the plan is a target beside the plan's own, the chat that started the review, the reviewer and a new session. A refused send, or a turn that ends with no version, readies the plan on the version reviewed; the review stays closed.
24. **Implemented, interrupted, discarded.** An implementation is implemented once its last turn ended well, its session runs no worker and no question card of its turns is open — read after the turn's items are stored; a failed or stopped turn makes it interrupted, and any later prompt of that chat continues it. A plan implemented, or discarded with Discard plan, is written so first, then each chat's request and line is settled, Plan released and PLAN READY let go of, and the plan deleted with its reviews (the maintainer; `AGENTS.md`'s hard rule). Leave plan mode, keep plan takes one chat out, its line `left`; a plan no chat takes part in stays listed, to be brought again.
25. **Removal and recovery.** Removing a chat takes it out of the plan; removing the writer readies the plan on the version before; removing, or marking Done, the chat of an implementation that runs nothing readies the plan on its version in the chats left, for any of them to carry out or discard, that chat no longer taking part — with no chat left it stays among the project's plans, to be brought again (the maintainer; it was discarded before) — and shelving it keeps it interrupted. Removing a plan or a project settles every operation through the coordinator first. At a launch a review waiting on its findings stays while its reviewer is listed, and is cancelled where it no longer is — the records started over, or a crash cut the reviewer's removal short (the maintainer); one found running, and a revision, ready the plan on its version; an implementation is implemented only where its last turn's success is on the disk and no card of it is open, else interrupted — or, where its chat is no longer listed, ready again as at that chat's removal, an interrupted one's too (the maintainer); a plan found implemented or cancelled is finished and deleted. Nothing is sent again. A write that fails leaves the plan as it was, every conflicting action refused meanwhile.

The quit question distinguishes chats waiting for a ready plan's decision from active replies or sub-agents. It says that saved plans stay ready for review after restarting and nothing runs automatically; active replies and sub-agents stop. The Main page and native fallback use the same summary counts and words. Quitting still asks only while a session runs, including a provider blocked on a plan decision; idle saved plans alone add no question.

The plan tab's controls: a compact black artifact bar shows Plan, an always-present version selector (disabled for one version), information on hover or focus (creation time, provider, model, originating chat), Copy of the entire selected source version, and a rightmost trash icon that uses the existing removal confirmation. Drafting and unfinished states remain visible. There is no folder action for a managed plan.

## What is left out

- **Beyond M22e's review:** several reviewers at once; clustering findings across reviews; a finding's questions as a record of their own; a review changed by a plain-composer turn; a reviewer narrower than the reading mode; and the CLIs' own schema options.
- **A plan workflow of fat-g's own** (`planModeInstructions`, `developer_instructions`), the Spec sheet and Milestone flows, editing a plan's text, and a plan exported into a project's folders, which would amend [0015](0015-no-writes-into-project-folders.md).
- **The model's own `EnterPlanMode`**, and the checklist tools, which stay as M20a left them.
- **The mode Claude's request proposes:** a mode never changes from a card ([0027](0027-execution-permissions.md), items 16 and 25), so Execute's `allow` carries no update.

## Consequences

- **A second store of content.** A plan's text is conversation content kept outside the conversation store, in `plans/`, until the user removes it; [0019](0019-conversation-store.md)'s "in the conversation store alone" reads "and a plan's text in the plan store". Removing a session leaves its plans, by design: they are the project's.
- **Codex sessions ask for an experimental API.** Every session's handshake asks for it, whether or not a plan turn ever runs, and what it unlocks may change between Codex versions. The best-effort fallback costs the account its plans and nothing else.
- **Claude's plan files stay where Claude writes them.** In a plan turn the model saves its plan in the account's configuration folder, which fat-g never reads ([0017](0017-provider-instances.md)): those files are neither read nor removed by fat-g, and `PRIVACY.md` says so.
- **A brought plan is sent to another account.** Bringing a plan into a chat of another provider or account sends its whole text to that account, at the user's press and with no notice of fat-g's.
- **A plan turn spends the user's plan,** and so does each feedback; Execute is a turn as any other.
- **A version-6 conversation store is refused as older** until Start fresh ([0014](0014-format-compatibility-starts-at-first-release.md)).

## Revisit when

- Codex makes `collaborationMode` stable, or its handshake no longer needs the experimental API: drop the field from the handshake and the fallback with it.
- Claude's `ExitPlanMode` request carries permission suggestions, or a later CLI no longer goes back to its earlier mode after Execute's `allow`: K1 runs again before the captures are trusted.
- A CLI reports its plan other than over the stream fat-g reads, a file alone say: plans stop for that provider rather than reading the file.
- A CLI's answers stop closing with the findings block the instruction asks for, or a later model renumbers its findings across rounds: the instruction's next version, with the spikes run again.
