# 0024 — A session's work: kept as reported, bounded, under its session

> **Rule:** what a session does between its messages — the files it reads, the commands it runs, the edits it reports, the tools it calls, the sub-agents it starts — is conversation content. fat-g keeps each as an item of the turn it happened in, as the provider reported it and never as the file system reads now, cut to fixed bounds on the way in, in the conversation store alone. A sub-agent's items and messages are kept under its id in the turn of its session that started it. Recording it sends nothing new to either CLI and grants nothing: what a session may do, and what is granted of what it asks, is [0027](0027-execution-permissions.md)'s.

## Context

- Until M20a the chat showed what was said and one line of what was being done, the action line. What a turn did was dropped at the adapter: [0019](0019-conversation-store.md) kept no command line and no tool output, because nothing recorded them.
- Both CLIs report each thing a session does as an item with a start and an end: Codex as `item/started` and `item/completed` of a typed item, Claude as a `tool_use` block and the `tool_result` that answers it. Both report a sub-agent's work on frames that name it. The shapes are in chat-protocols.md (private research note) and in the captures the replay fakes hold.
- A tool's output has no bound of the CLI's own that fat-g can rely on, a reported edit is what the tool wrote at the time and not what the file holds later, and a path a tool names may hold the user's name.
- Neither CLI sends the text of its reasoning under the launches fat-g makes (M20a, K1; M19, K2).

## Decision

1. **An activity is an item of its turn.** One of a closed list of types — `read`, `search`, `command`, `edit`, `mcp`, `web-search`, `worker`, `wait`, `thinking`, `checklist`, `other` — with the provider's name for it, one line of summary, its input, its output, the files it names, an edit's changes, a checklist's steps, its status, its exit code and its duration. A type the adapter does not know is `other`, kept by its name. The agent events are at version 5: `activity-started` and `activity-ended`, and a message, a message's delta and both of these may name the worker whose item it is. *(M21, C19, C72:)* one tool's use is no activity: Claude's `AskUserQuestion` — its `tool_use` and its result make no item, since the question's card ([0026](0026-questions.md)) stands at that place, and the result is read only as the CLI's word that it took the answer.
2. **As reported, never as the file system reads now.** Nothing is read from disk to fill an item in. An edit's changes are what the provider reported with the item, and the chat says when; a file an item names is a reference, opened only at the user's press and under [0022](0022-reading-project-files.md)'s rule. But for an image a finished read names, which the chat draws under the group's line by 0022's item 15: read to be drawn, never to fill the item in, and kept nowhere. Codex's `imageView` — a picture its model looked at — is such a read of its file.
3. **Bounded on the way in.** A name 256 units, a summary one line of 2,000, an input 10,000, an output 20,000 — kept as its first and its last halves, with how much was left out — a thinking text 20,000, an edit's change 20,000 and 20 changes, a path 2,048, 200 files, a checklist 100 steps of 500, and the body as a whole 200,000, past which fields give way in a fixed order. What was cut is said with the item. A session's line from its CLI may be 4 MiB; a longer one is dropped and counted, and nothing of it is read. *(M22, C67, C71; [0027](0027-execution-permissions.md), item 22:)* of a longer one the first 512 bytes are read for two things alone — whether it is a request, and its request id — by a bounded scan with no parse, and are then let go: nothing else of it is read, kept or logged. A request so read is refused on the wire by that id, with a notice naming the line's size, and its turn goes on. One that is or may be a request and whose id cannot be read stops everything its process runs — the session's turn and its workers by the session's Stop, each running aside by the aside's own — with a notice. With nothing running, and for any other line, it is dropped and counted as before. A change within its own content bound whose escaped line passes 4 MiB takes this path: a line's limit and a content's bound are two things.
4. **A status is the provider's word, or none.** `running` from the start; `completed` or `failed` at the end, by the provider's own flag, exit code or status; `unfinished` where the end never came by the time its turn ended, or fat-g did. A completion that arrives after its turn's end still ends its item. The store knows nothing that runs: what it holds as running reads unfinished, and only the session that holds an item open says it runs. A duration is the provider's where it gives one, and fat-g's own between the two frames otherwise, and says which. *(M22, C76; [0027](0027-execution-permissions.md), item 19:)* a fifth status, `declined`: the action was refused leave and never ran, which is no failure of the agent's. It is set from Codex's own `declined` status of the item, live and from history; on Claude from the result of a tool use the user denied on its card, and from the CLI's `system/permission_denied` frame, which names a rule of the user's own configuration as the cause. With it goes who declined, where the adapter knows: the user, or a rule. An action that was allowed, or that ran under a grant or a mode, keeps the status its provider gave. A declined item has ended, and is not read again as unfinished.
5. **A sub-agent's work is kept under its id, in its session's conversation.** Its items and messages are filed in the turn of the session's that started it, though that turn may have ended, and are in no answer of the session's own: they are read by the worker's id. The item of the session's own that started it — Claude's `Agent` tool use, Codex's sub-agent announcement — is paired with the worker, carries the agent list's label for it, and ends as the worker ends. Removing a session removes its workers' items with it. *(M106, C45:)* Claude sends a foreground sub-agent's tool uses on its frames and none of its text: where a sub-agent sent no text of its own, its report — the summary its notification carries, its final text — is kept as its last message, under its id, before its item ends.
6. **Thinking is kept and not drawn.** A thinking item holds what the provider emits of its reasoning, labelled as the provider's, never made up. Neither CLI sends a text today, so the item has no row and counts in no summary line; how thinking is shown is later presentation work.
7. **A checklist is the agent's own list, and never a plan.** One item a turn, replaced at each update, completed with its turn. Codex reports one only where the user's own configuration enables its `update_plan` tool, which fat-g does not switch on ([0018](0018-codex-sessions.md), item 4); Claude's launch offers no such tool ([0020](0020-claude-chat-sessions.md), item 5). Whether fat-g should enable either is M22b's question.
8. **Nothing new is sent, and nothing is granted.** Codex's two method sets and its handshake are as they were; Claude's launch, its four tools, its four sends and its two answers are as they were. Reading more of the frames a CLI already sends is all that changed. *(M21:)* true of M20a; the tools are five and the answers three since M21 ([0020](0020-claude-chat-sessions.md), items 3–5; [0026](0026-questions.md)), and an answer to a question still grants no action. *(M22:)* still true of recording a session's work: reading more of a frame sends nothing and grants nothing. What a session is sent, and what is granted of what it asks, changed with M22 and is [0027](0027-execution-permissions.md)'s: Codex's sets are three, its start, turn and resume name the policy of the chat's mode, and its approval requests are answered from the user's decision ([0018](0018-codex-sessions.md), items 2, 3 and 7); Claude's sends are seven, its answers four, and its tools eight under a mode that acts ([0020](0020-claude-chat-sessions.md), items 2 to 5).
9. **In the store alone.** Activity reaches the conversation store's files and the Main window, and nothing else: no log, no summary, no app state, and never the Pet. Main says of it only kinds and counts.
10. **The history check completes, and never doubles.** A turn with an unfinished activity is read again as one with an unfinished message is. A history item whose provider id matches a stored activity completes it in place; one with no match is added in the provider's order; a stored activity the history lacks stays as it is. Codex's history holds no checklist; Claude has no history.

**Returned-image amendment:** a Codex `imageGeneration` item still has its ordinary activity, but its bounded inline result also reaches main as a separate `image-returned` event (contract revision 14). Main retains a verified image through the existing attachment ownership, or writes an explicit failure notice; no encoded payload falls into an activity's text or a log. Chat lines have a 32 MiB envelope; the existing shared connection allows that size only for a pending history reply's own id, keeping all other replies and frames at their previous limits. Image content has its own smaller bounds. See the implementation note (private); no new request, permission or provider-file read is introduced.

## What is left out


- Approving or refusing what a session asks to do: M22 (M21 answers a question the session asks, and approves nothing). A session still runs read-only, and every request but a question is refused. *(M22 built it, in [0027](0027-execution-permissions.md): a session runs under its chat's mode, and a request for leave is a card.)*
- Plans, and a plan's steps as a plan: M22b. A checklist is drawn as a task list.
- How thinking is shown, and customizing what the activity shows: later presentation work.
- A search over what a session did.

## Consequences

- `conversations.sqlite` now holds command lines, paths, tool output and results in clear, and a path holds the user's name. [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md) says so. The leak scans take the captures' tool outputs as needles.
- The store is at version 3: a `workerId` column on an item, with its index. No migration: a version-2 store is refused as older ([0014](0014-format-compatibility-starts-at-first-release.md)).
- A page and a window are counted in entries — a message, a notice, or a turn's consecutive activities — and in size, so a turn of many tools is not a page of its own.
- A new provider reports activity through the same two events, and its sub-agents' items by the worker's id; `tests/sessions/facet-contract.ts` holds it to their order, and nothing in the shared code names a provider.
